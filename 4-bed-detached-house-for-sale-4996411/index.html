SALE OF FOUR BEDROOM DETACHED COUNTRY HOUSE ON C. 0.75 ACRE AT SKAHANAGH BEG, BANTRY, WEST CORK.

We are delighted to promote the sale of this commanding modern built country house of high spec finish and enjoying elevated south facing views across the Mealagh Valley.

The beautifully finished residence was designed and constructed as a family home in 2014. The thoughtful layout allows for bright and spacious living and reception rooms plus a large en-suite ground floor bedroom. The mature c. 0.75 acre plot has good fertile ground, mature trees and shrubs plus a very well laid out courtyard and a large raised bed with extensive dry stone walling and built in stone seat.

A notable and recent addition on the grounds is a cabin which is a detached timber frame building which can be used for creative pursuits or as a home office as it has light, power and separate wifi. It commands stunning views across the valley.

The levels of insulation are immediately noticeable upon entering the residence and another addition recently is a new water filtration system. Every room boasts superb countryside scenery and great privacy. There are national schools in close proximity at nearby Dromclough NS and Kealkil NS. Bantry town centre is within 10 minute drive and Cork can be easily accessed via Kealkil. Viewing is highly recommended!
